# Disclaimer

**Katipunan** is an academic and educational game project inspired by selected events and figures from the Philippine Revolution.

The game is a **fictionalized and simplified interpretation of historical events** created for educational and entertainment purposes. Some gameplay mechanics, character interactions, events, dialogue, and representations may not accurately reflect the exact historical circumstances.

The game is **not intended to replace historical sources, academic research, or formal historical accounts**. Players are encouraged to consult reliable historical references for a more accurate understanding of the Philippine Revolution.

Some characters, locations, events, and other historical elements are based on real people and events. Any fictionalized elements are used solely to support the game's gameplay and storytelling.
